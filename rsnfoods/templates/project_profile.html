This is a demo, not a real shop

Django Food is a demo project built to show how a complete food delivery system is put together. Every restaurant, food item, price and order you see is sample data. Nothing here is sold, cooked or delivered, and no real money moves.

The structure is real though: separate logins and screens for each role, a working cart, orders that move through stages, invoices and a sales report. The same base can grow into a real service by adding the pieces listed further down this page.

  • TypeDemo project (v0.0.0)
  • StackDjango, PostgreSQL, Bootstrap
  • DataSample restaurants and foods
  • PaymentsSimulated (cash on delivery flow)

Who uses it

Four roles, each with its own side

01

Customer

  • Browse restaurants and menus
  • Heart foods into favorites
  • Add to cart, remove, clear
  • Checkout with a delivery address
  • Follow order status, open invoice
  • English and Bangla
02

Restaurant owner

  • Add and edit restaurants and foods
  • Upload photos
  • Review and confirm new orders
  • Sales page: revenue, last 7 days, top foods
  • Print invoices
03

Delivery person

  • See assigned orders
  • Mark an order as delivering, then delivered
  • Update payment status on handover
  • View customer address and phone
04

Admin

  • Manage users and roles
  • Choose which restaurants and foods appear on the home page
  • Inspect every order
  • Uses the built-in Django admin

How an order moves

One order, three people

1Customer adds food to the cart
2Checkout creates the order
3Owner confirms it
4A courier is assigned automatically
5Courier delivers and collects payment
6Sales report and invoice update

The courier is picked automatically: the delivery person with the fewest active orders gets the next one, so work spreads out evenly.
